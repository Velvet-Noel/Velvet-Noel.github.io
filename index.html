Pre-tied, then fluffed

Materials

Soft velvet, honest expectations.

The Statement Bow is a decorative velvet bow with long streamers. It is not a licensed designer piece, and it is not weatherproof for unsheltered outdoor use in rain or snow. Bring it in if the weather turns, and keep it away from open flames.

  1. Unpack and gently pull the loops into shape.
  2. If there are creases, use a low steam iron held above the fabric — do not press hard on the pile.
  3. Use the back tie to fasten it to a wreath hook, a door knocker, a tree top, or a banister.
  4. After the holidays, fold it loosely and store it in a dry place.
