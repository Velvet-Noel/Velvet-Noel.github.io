Oversized on purpose

The bow is about 50 cm wide, with tails that fall close to 180 cm. It is meant to be the focal point on a door, a tree, or a banister — not a stocking trim.

Pre-tied, then fluffed

It arrives folded. Give the loops a shake, smooth the tails, and steam lightly if the packing left a crease. A hanging tie is sewn on the back.

One decoration, many places

Hang it on the front door, use it as a tree topper, or tie it to the staircase. Pack it flat after the season and use it again next year.

No plugs, no fuss

There are no lights, batteries, or liquids. It ships as a soft, lightweight parcel, which is why it can travel worldwide more easily than décor that needs a power adaptor.

Materials

Soft velvet, honest expectations.

The Statement Bow is a decorative velvet bow with long streamers. The tree skirt is a soft, ruffled velvet floor piece, not a rigid or fireproof tree collar. Neither is a licensed designer piece, and neither is weatherproof for unsheltered outdoor use in rain or snow. Bring the bow in if the weather turns, and keep both away from open flames.

  1. Unpack and gently pull the bow loops into shape, or shake out the skirt ruffles.
  2. If there are creases, use a low steam iron held above the fabric — do not press hard on the pile.
  3. Use the back tie to fasten the bow to a wreath hook, a door knocker, a tree top, or a banister. Wrap the skirt around the trunk and smooth it on the floor.
  4. After the holidays, fold each piece loosely and store it in a dry place.
